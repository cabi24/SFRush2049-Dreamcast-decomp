## Research limits

The patches are historical near misses against round-four starting source
12f77c5 (source/include/map identical to published 8c93c91). They are not a
recommendation to apply them to master. Shifted equality and matching instruction
shapes with different call/pool displacements earn no credit. All proposed future
changes require new evidence, semantic review, frozen compiler/linker replay,
strict fixed-address emitted-byte audit and preservation of all accepted spans.

Presentation's three retained candidates were independently replayed on clean
baseline translation units. Other combined exploratory trials retain their
noncausal-attribution caveat; do not present those deltas as isolated improvements.
Warning/circuit declarations, seven audio bodies and SetClutch remain research.

Only normalized public-safe rows and source text are included. Original private
ledgers (including first-difference snippets), full proof directories, target bytes,
assembly dumps, binaries, credentials and local workspace paths are excluded.
Scoped historical lane audits are not a full-project integration claim. MAT3/N64
are reconstructed semantic references; Dreamcast target evidence decides acceptance.
No whole-game runtime test, native/Linux raw-artifact identity, automatic merge or
CI monitoring is claimed.
