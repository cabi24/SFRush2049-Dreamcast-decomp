## Preserved research and remaining blocker

[Draft research PR 6](https://github.com/cabi24/SFRush2049-Dreamcast-decomp/pull/6)
preserves the conservative ledger, four source-only research deltas, native layout
findings and a bounded synthetic compiler-context experiment. Research is **not**
accepted source: known regressions, unbound tables, out-of-map extents and combined-build
limits are explicit. Patches apply separately and together to the accepted tree;
that is not a claim that the combined research build is matching or regression-free.

The controlled SHC/LNK experiment used unchanged frozen flags. A same-TU caller used
a direct branch; separate real objects and DEFINE-only mini-linking used byte-identical
indirect caller output at the same nearby address. Default LNK did not relax that
case. This supports investigating genuine source completeness and original compilation
groups, not adding padding or blindly repeating source shapes. The wrapper's repeated
internal dos_src unit name also had to be avoided for the synthetic multi-object test;
production tooling was not changed by that experiment. Synthetic controls contribute
zero targets or matching credit.

Further progress on the blocked remainder needs specific new evidence or a focused
compiler/unit-layout investigation rather than another unchanged variant. The requested
500-target goal remains partially unfulfilled; the delivered count is 473.
